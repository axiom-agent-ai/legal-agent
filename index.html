See It In Action

A startup NDA landed on your desk. Here's what Senior Counsel flags before you sign.

Input: "Series A investor NDA. Standard 2-year term. Check for problematic clauses."
🔴 Non-Compete — Section 4.2
"Recipient shall not engage in any business competitive with the Disclosing Party for a period of 3 years following termination."
🔴 HIGH RISK: 3-year non-compete is unreasonable for a standard NDA.
Standard investor NDA = 1 year maximum. 3 years is venture-backed territory with proper compensation. Without consideration, this is unenforceable — but that doesn't stop them from suing you to intimidate.
🟡 Assignment of IP — Section 6.1
"All ideas, concepts, and information shared by the Disclosing Party shall become the sole property of the Disclosing Party."
🟡 REVIEW: Overly broad. Could capture your general industry knowledge.
Push for carve-out: "general industry knowledge and publicly available information are excluded." Without this, any industry insight you share during diligence could be claimed as theirs.
🟢 Confidential Information Definition — Section 1.3
"Excludes information that: (a) is publicly available; (b) was known prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed."
🟢 STANDARD: Standard exclusions. No surprises here.
🔴 Termination — Section 8.1
"This Agreement may be terminated by either party with 30 days written notice."
🔴 HIGH RISK: One-sided termination clause.
Standard NDAs are bilateral. If they terminate, their confidentiality obligations end but your obligations continue. Add: "Either party's right to terminate is subject to the survival of confidentiality obligations for a period of 3 years following termination."
🟡 Non-Solicitation — Section 9.4
"Recipient shall not solicit employees of the Disclosing Party for a period of 2 years."
🟡 REVIEW: Standard but broad. Narrow to key personnel only.
"Employees" includes all staff, not just executives. Push for "senior management and direct reports" only. Also check if it applies to contractors.
Verdict: 3 red flags, 2 yellow flags.

Recommended action: Return with red-line markup. The non-compete and termination clause are deal-breakers without modification. The IP clause is negotiable. Do not sign as-is.
